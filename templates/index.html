What you get

Each figure follows a published rule. See the formulas.

  • Fab WIP and shipmentsWafers at the foundry by product and stage, and wafers shipped.
  • Late fab lotsLots projected out after their committed date.
  • OSAT WIP by operationSort and Bump in wafers; Assembly, Test and Packing in dies.
  • Duplicate removalFive rules for transfer, finished and stale rows, each removed row explained.
  • Ambiguous part namesGeneric OSAT names assigned to a product through fab lot numbers.
  • Excess & obsoleteOld date codes and downgraded bins.
  • Capacity checkActive WIP compared with wafers shipped × gross die per wafer.
  • Lot lead timeProcess and wait days per stage for every mother lot.
  • Demand forecastStatistical forecast blended with customer forecasts, with accuracy and bias.
  • Module-level supply planWafer starts and loads on fab, bump, sort, assembly and test, netted against stock and WIP.
  • Tester capacityATE hours needed versus available per tester type and month.
  • Monthly B2B packageRolling FAB and OSAT forecasts with time fences and a commit sheet.
